# Retail Business Performance Analysis

A data analytics project that analyzes retail transaction data to evaluate business performance, product profitability, customer behavior, regional performance, sales trends, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

The project was developed using **Power BI, Power Query, and DAX** to transform, analyze, and visualize the data into an interactive business dashboard.

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify actionable insights that can support business decision-making and improve sales and profitability.

---

## Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer the following questions:

1. How is the overall business performing in terms of sales and profit?
2. Which product categories and sub-categories generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which region has the strongest sales and profit performance?
4. How do sales and profit change over time?
5. Which customers contribute the highest sales and profit?
6. Is there a relationship between discount levels and profit?
7. Which products perform best and which products generate the largest losses?

## Analysis

### 1. Overall Business Performance

The overall business performance was evaluated using key performance indicators (KPIs), including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin

### 2. Product Analysis

Product performance was analyzed at both category and sub-category levels to identify the main contributors to sales and profitability.

Key analyses:

* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Sales by Sub-Category
* Profit by Sub-Category

**Key Finding:**
Furniture generates the highest sales, while Technology generates the highest profit. Bookcases is the top-performing sub-category by sales, while Copiers generates the highest profit among sub-categories.

### 3. Geographic Analysis

Sales and profit performance were compared across regions.

**Key Finding:**
The West region generates the highest sales and profit, making it the strongest-performing region.

### 4. Time Analysis

Sales and profit trends were analyzed over time to identify changes in business performance.

**Key Finding:**
Sales show a consistent increasing trend over the years. Profit declined in 2012 but recovered afterward and continued to increase in subsequent years.

### 5. Customer Analysis

Customer performance was analyzed based on sales and profit contribution.

**Key Finding:**
Anna Geyman has the highest total sales, while Reymon Brunch generates the highest total profit. This indicates that the customer with the highest sales is not necessarily the most profitable customer.

### 6. Discount Analysis

The relationship between discount levels and profit was analyzed using:

* Discount vs. Profit Scatter Plot
* Average Profit by Discount

**Key Finding:**
Higher discount levels tend to be associated with lower profitability. However, this analysis indicates an association rather than proving that discounts directly cause lower profit.

### 7. Product Performance

Individual product performance was evaluated using:

* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Products by Profit
* Bottom 10 Products by Profit

**Key Findings:**

* **Highest Sales:** Riverside Palais Royal Lawyers Bookcase, Royal Cherry Finish
* **Highest Profit:** Canon ImageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier
* **Largest Loss:** Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Triple Head Print

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several recommendations were identified:

1. **Optimize Discount Strategy**
   Evaluate and control high discount levels to reduce potential impacts on profitability.

2. **Focus on Profitable Products**
   Prioritize products and categories that generate strong profit while reviewing products with low or negative profitability.

3. **Strengthen Regional Performance**
   Maintain the strong performance of the West region while identifying opportunities to improve other regions.

4. **Evaluate Customers Based on Profitability**
   Consider both sales and profit when evaluating customer value rather than relying on sales alone.

5. **Monitor Sales and Profit Trends**
   Continuously monitor business performance to identify changes and potential declines in profitability.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates positive long-term business performance, with overall sales and profit generally increasing over time.

Furniture leads in sales, while Technology leads in profit, and the West region demonstrates the strongest regional performance. The analysis also indicates that higher discount levels tend to be associated with lower profitability.

Overall, the findings emphasize the importance of balancing sales growth with profitability to support better data-driven business decisions.
